Orange Juice

Ingredients:

4-6 large oranges (for about 2 cups of juice)
1-2 tablespoons sugar or honey (optional, to taste)
Ice cubes (optional)
Mint leaves or orange slices (for garnish)

Continue...

Instructions


1. Prepare the Oranges: Peel and Segment: Peel the oranges, removing as much of the white pith as possible. Cut them into segments. If you have a citrus juicer, you can simply cut the oranges in half and juice them directly.
2. Juice the Oranges: Manual Juicer: If using a manual citrus juicer, press the orange segments to extract the juice. You can do this by hand or with a dedicated juicer. Electric Juicer: If using an electric citrus juicer, simply place the orange halves on the juicer and press down to extract the juice.
3. Sweeten (Optional): Taste and Adjust: Taste the juice. If you prefer it sweeter, you can stir in sugar or honey to taste. If the juice is too tart, adjust the sweetness to your liking.
4. Serve: Chill: If desired, chill the juice in the refrigerator before serving. Add Ice: Serve the juice over ice cubes for a cold and refreshing drink.
5. Garnish (Optional): Decorate: Garnish with mint leaves or orange slices for a decorative touch.

Tips:

Use Fresh Oranges: For the best flavor, use fresh, ripe oranges. Navel oranges are a popular choice for their sweetness.
Strain if Desired: If you prefer pulp-free juice, strain it through a fine mesh sieve or cheesecloth before serving.
Add a Twist: For a flavor twist, you can add a splash of lemon juice or a few mint leaves to the orange juice.

Enjoy your fresh, homemade orange juice! It’s a perfect start to the day or a refreshing treat anytime.
