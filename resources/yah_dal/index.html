Ingredients

For the Dal:
1 cup black lentils (urad dal)
1/4 cup kidney beans (rajma)
4 cups water
1/2 tsp turmeric powder
1 tsp salt

For the Gravy:
2 tbsp ghee or oil
1 large onion, finely chopped
2-3 green chilies, slit
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
2 large tomatoes, pureed
1/2 tsp red chili powder
1/2 tsp turmeric powder
1 tsp garam masala
1/2 tsp cumin seeds
1/2 tsp coriander powder
1/2 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
1 cup heavy cream (or a mix of cream and milk)
Salt to taste Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Dal Makhani Recipee

Instructions


1. Preparing the Dal:
Soak the Lentils and Beans:
Rinse the black lentils and kidney beans. Soak them in water for at least 8 hours or overnight.
2. Cook the Lentils and Beans:
Drain the soaked lentils and beans. In a large pot or pressure cooker, add the lentils, kidney beans, 4 cups of water, turmeric powder, and salt.
Cook until they are soft and tender. If using a pressure cooker, cook for about 15-20 minutes after the first whistle. If using a regular pot, it may take 45-60 minutes.
3. Preparing the Gravy:
Heat the Oil/Ghee:
In a separate pan, heat the ghee or oil over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter.
4. Sauté Onions and Spices:
Add the chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a couple of minutes until the raw smell disappears.
Add Tomatoes and Spices:
Stir in the tomato puree. Cook until the oil starts to separate from the masala.
Add red chili powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Mix well and cook for another 5 minutes.
Combine with Dal:
Add the cooked lentils and beans to the tomato-onion mixture. Mix well.
Adjust the consistency by adding water if needed. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
Add Cream and Finish:
Stir in the heavy cream and kasuri methi. Cook for an additional 5-10 minutes.
Adjust salt to taste.
4. Garnishing and Serving:
Garnish with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.

Enjoy your Dal Makhani! It’s a comforting dish that’s perfect for a family meal or special occasion.
