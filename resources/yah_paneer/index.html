Ingredients

For the Paneer Marinade:
250 grams paneer (cubed)
1/2 cup plain yogurt
1/2 tsp turmeric powder
1/2 tsp red chili powder
1/2 tsp garam masala
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
Salt to taste
For the Gravy:
2 tbsp butter
1 tbsp oil (optional, for extra richness)
1 large onion, finely chopped
2-3 green chilies, slit (adjust to taste)
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
2 large tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
1/2 cup tomato puree (optional, for a richer gravy)
1/2 tsp turmeric powder
1 tsp red chili powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp cumin powder
1 tsp garam masala
1/2 cup fresh cream
1/4 cup milk (adjust for consistency)
1/2 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
Salt to taste
Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

- Butter Paneer Masala Recipee

Instructions


Preparing the Paneer:
Marinate the Paneer:
In a bowl, mix the yogurt, turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, ginger-garlic paste, and salt.
Add the paneer cubes and gently mix to coat them well. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
Preparing the Gravy:
Heat Butter and Oil:
In a large pan, heat 2 tablespoons of butter (and 1 tablespoon of oil, if using) over medium heat.
Sauté Onions and Spices:
Add the finely chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
Add Ginger-Garlic Paste:
Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another 1-2 minutes until the raw smell disappears.
Cook Tomatoes:
Add the chopped tomatoes and tomato puree (if using). Cook until the tomatoes are soft and the oil starts to separate from the mixture.
Add Spices:
Stir in the turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and garam masala. Mix well and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
Add Cream and Milk:
Lower the heat and stir in the fresh cream and milk. Mix well to combine and cook for a few minutes to get a smooth, creamy texture.
Add Paneer:
Gently add the marinated paneer cubes to the gravy. Mix carefully to coat the paneer with the sauce.
Simmer and Finish:
Simmer the dish for about 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the paneer to absorb the gravy.
Add the kasuri methi and adjust salt to taste.
Garnishing and Serving:
Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and an extra dollop of butter if desired.
Serve hot with naan, roti, paratha, or rice.

Enjoy your creamy, delicious Butter Paneer Masala! It’s a comforting dish that's sure to impress.
