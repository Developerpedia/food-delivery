Kalakand Recipe

Ingredients For the Kalakand:

1 liter (4 cups) whole milk
1/2 cup sugar
1/2 teaspoon lemon juice or vinegar (for curdling)
1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder
2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
1/4 cup chopped nuts (almonds, cashews, or pistachios)

Instructions

Prepare Paneer (Chenna):

1 Boil Milk: Heat the milk in a large pan over medium heat, stirring occasionally.
2 Curdle the Milk: Once the milk comes to a boil, add lemon juice or vinegar gradually, stirring continuously. The milk will start to curdle. If it doesn’t, add a bit more lemon juice or vinegar.
3 Drain: Pour the curdled milk through a cheesecloth or fine sieve to separate the curds (chenna) from the whey. Rinse the curds under cold water to remove the acidic taste.
4 Press: Gather the cheesecloth and squeeze out excess water. Press the curds on a flat surface with a heavy object for about 30 minutes to remove more moisture.

Continue...

Prepare the Kalakand Mixture:

1 Heat Ghee: In a non-stick pan, heat the ghee over medium heat.
2 Cook Chenna: Add the pressed chenna to the pan. Cook, stirring continuously, until the chenna starts to cook and changes color (about 5-7 minutes).
3 Add Sugar and Flavorings: Add sugar, cardamom powder, and saffron strands (if using) to the pan. Stir well to combine. Continue to cook the mixture, stirring constantly, until the sugar dissolves and the mixture thickens.
4 Cook Until Set: Cook the mixture until it starts to leave the sides of the pan and forms a soft dough-like consistency (about 10-15 minutes).

Set and Garnish:

1 Pour and Level: Transfer the mixture to a greased plate or tray. Flatten and level it with a spatula to a thickness of about 1/2 inch.
2 Garnish: Sprinkle chopped nuts on top while the mixture is still warm, pressing them gently into the surface.
3 Cool and Cut: Let the Kalakand cool completely. Once set, cut into squares or desired shapes.

Enjoy your homemade Kalakand, a delightful and rich Indian sweet treat!
