Ingredients

For the Rice:
1 1/2 cups basmati rice
2-3 cups water
1-2 bay leaves
2-3 green cardamom pods
4-5 cloves
1 small cinnamon stick
1 teaspoon salt
For the Vegetables:
2 tablespoons oil or ghee
1 large onion, thinly sliced
2-3 green chilies, slit (adjust to taste)
1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
1 large tomato, chopped
1/2 cup chopped carrots
1/2 cup chopped potatoes
1/2 cup green beans, chopped
1/2 cup peas
1/2 cup yogurt (curd)
1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped
Biryani Masala:
1 tablespoon biryani masala (store-bought or homemade)
1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
1 teaspoon red chili powder
1 teaspoon coriander powder
1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
Salt to taste
For Garnish:
Fried onions (store-bought or homemade)
Fresh coriander leaves
Fresh mint leaves

Veg Biryani Recipee

Instructions

Preparing the Rice:
Rinse and Soak Rice:
Rinse the basmati rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
Cook the Rice:
In a large pot, bring 2-3 cups of water to a boil. Add bay leaves, cardamom pods, cloves, cinnamon stick, and salt.
Add the soaked and drained rice to the boiling water. Cook until the rice is 70-80% cooked (the rice should still have a bite to it). Drain and set aside.
Preparing the Vegetables and Masala:
Heat Oil/Ghee:
In a large, heavy-bottomed pan or pressure cooker, heat oil or ghee over medium heat.
Sauté Onions and Spices:
Add the sliced onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions are golden brown.
Add Ginger-Garlic Paste:
Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1-2 minutes until the raw smell disappears.
Cook Tomatoes and Vegetables:
Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn soft.
Add the chopped carrots, potatoes, green beans, peas, and bell peppers (if using). Stir and cook for a few minutes.
Add Spices and Yogurt:
Stir in the biryani masala, turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix well.
Add the yogurt and cook until the vegetables are tender and the masala has cooked through.
Layering and Cooking:
Layer the Biryani:
In a heavy-bottomed pan, start by spreading a layer of the cooked vegetable mixture. Then, layer half of the cooked rice over the vegetables.
Sprinkle half of the chopped coriander and mint leaves over the rice.
Add another layer of the remaining vegetable mixture and top with the remaining rice.
Sprinkle the rest of the coriander and mint leaves on top.
Dum Cooking:
Cover the pan tightly with a lid. You can also seal the edges with a dough (made from flour and water) to trap the steam.
Cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together (this method is called "dum" cooking).
Serve:
Gently fluff the biryani with a fork before serving.
Garnish with fried onions, additional fresh coriander, and mint leaves.

Enjoy your flavorful Veg Biryani! It’s a comforting and aromatic dish that’s sure to be a hit with everyone.
