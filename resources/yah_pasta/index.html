Ingredients

For the Pasta:
200 grams (about 2 cups) pasta (penne, fusilli, or your choice)
1 tablespoon oil
1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
1 large onion, finely chopped
2-3 green chilies, slit (adjust to taste)
1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
2 large tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, peas, corn), chopped
1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
1 teaspoon red chili powder
1 teaspoon garam masala
1 teaspoon coriander powder
1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
1/2 teaspoon chaat masala (optional)
Salt to taste
1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
1/4 cup fresh cream or milk (optional for creaminess)

Pav Bhaji Recipee

Instructions

Preparing the Pasta:
Cook the Pasta:
Boil a pot of salted water. Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
Making the Masala:
Heat Oil:
In a large pan, heat oil over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
Sauté Onions and Spices:
Add the finely chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
Add Ginger-Garlic Paste:
Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another 1-2 minutes until the raw smell disappears.
Cook Tomatoes:
Add the chopped or pureed tomatoes. Cook until the tomatoes are soft and the oil starts to separate from the mixture.
Add Vegetables and Spices:
Add the chopped vegetables and cook for a few minutes until they start to soften.
Stir in turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix well.
Combine with Pasta:
Add the cooked pasta to the pan and mix gently to combine with the masala.
If using, stir in the fresh cream or milk to make the dish creamier. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, allowing the pasta to absorb the flavors.
Finish and Garnish:
Sprinkle chaat masala (if using) and chopped coriander leaves. Mix well and adjust seasoning to taste.
Serve:
Serve hot, garnished with extra coriander leaves or a sprinkle of grated cheese if desired.

This Indian Masala Pasta combines the best of both worlds, offering a unique twist on traditional pasta with bold Indian spices. Enjoy!
