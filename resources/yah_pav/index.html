Ingredients

For the Bhaji (Vegetable Mash):
2 tbsp oil or butter
1 tsp cumin seeds
1 large onion, finely chopped
1 bell pepper, finely chopped
2-3 green chilies, finely chopped (adjust to taste)
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
2 large tomatoes, finely chopped
1 cup potatoes, boiled and mashed
1/2 cup cauliflower, finely chopped
1/2 cup green peas
1/2 cup carrots, finely chopped
2 tbsp Pav Bhaji Masala (available at Indian grocery stores)
1/2 tsp turmeric powder
1/2 tsp red chili powder
1/2 tsp cumin powder
1/2 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp sugar
Salt to taste
1-2 tbsp lemon juice
2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander leaves
Water as needed
For the Pav:
8-10 pav (bread rolls)
2 tbsp butter

Pav Bhaji Recipee

Instructions

Preparing the Bhaji:
Heat the Oil/Butter:
In a large pan or skillet, heat oil or butter over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
Sauté Onions and Spices:
Add the finely chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute.
Cook Tomatoes:
Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and the oil starts to separate from the mixture.
Add Vegetables:
Add the chopped bell pepper, boiled and mashed potatoes, cauliflower, green peas, and carrots. Mix well.
Add Spices:
Stir in the Pav Bhaji Masala, turmeric powder, red chili powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, and sugar. Mix everything well.
Simmer:
Add water to achieve the desired consistency (usually a thick but spreadable consistency). Simmer the mixture for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until all the vegetables are cooked through and the flavors are well blended. Mash and Finish:
Use a potato masher to mash the bhaji while it's cooking, ensuring it's smooth but still has some texture.
Stir in the lemon juice and fresh coriander leaves. Adjust salt to taste.
Preparing the Pav:
Butter the Pav:
Heat a tawa or skillet over medium heat. Add a little butter and place the pav on the tawa.
Toast the pav on both sides until they are golden and crispy.
Serve:
Serve the hot bhaji with the buttered pav. Optionally, you can garnish the bhaji with a dollop of butter and serve with lemon wedges, chopped onions, and coriander leaves on the side.

Enjoy your homemade Pav Bhaji! It’s a delightful, comfort food that's perfect for any time of the day.
