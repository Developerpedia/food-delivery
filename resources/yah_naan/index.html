Ingredients

For the Dough:
2 1/4 teaspoons (1 packet) active dry yeast
1/2 teaspoon sugar
1 cup warm water (110°F or 45°C)
3 1/2 to 4 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
1/2 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon baking soda
1/2 cup plain yogurt (curd)
1/4 cup milk
2 tablespoons ghee or melted butter
1 teaspoon salt
Optional for Garnishing:
Melted butter or ghee
Chopped fresh coriander leaves
Nigella seeds (kalonji) or sesame seeds
Minced garlic or grated cheese

Tandoori Naan Recipee

Instructions


Preparing the Dough:
Activate the Yeast:
In a small bowl, dissolve the sugar in warm water. Sprinkle the yeast over the water and let it sit for about 5-10 minutes, until it becomes frothy.
Mix Dry Ingredients:
In a large bowl, mix 3 1/2 cups of flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
Combine Ingredients:
Make a well in the center of the flour mixture. Add the yeast mixture, yogurt, milk, and ghee (or melted butter).
Mix until the dough starts coming together. You may need to add a bit more flour or water depending on the consistency of the dough.
Knead the Dough:
Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead for about 5-7 minutes, until it’s smooth and elastic.
Let the Dough Rise:
Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl. Cover with a damp cloth or plastic wrap and let it rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours, or until it has doubled in size.


Rolling and Cooking the Naan: Preheat the Oven/Stovetop:

If using an oven, preheat it to 475°F (245°C) and place a baking sheet or pizza stone inside. If using a stovetop, preheat a heavy-bottomed skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Prepare the Dough:
Punch down the risen dough and divide it into 8-10 equal portions. Roll each portion into a ball.
Roll Out the Naan:

On a lightly floured surface, roll out each ball into an oval or round shape, about 1/4 inch thick. You can sprinkle some flour or use a rolling pin to make it easier. Add Toppings (Optional):
If desired, sprinkle with chopped coriander leaves, nigella seeds, or minced garlic.
Cook the Naan:

Oven Method: Place the rolled-out naan on the preheated baking sheet or pizza stone. Bake for 2-3 minutes or until bubbles form and the naan starts to brown. Flip it and bake for another 1-2 minutes.
Stovetop Method: Place the rolled-out naan on the hot skillet. Cook for 1-2 minutes or until bubbles start to form. Flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown and cooked through. You can press it gently with a cloth to ensure even cooking.
Finish and Serve:
Brush the hot naan with melted butter or ghee. Optionally, garnish with additional chopped coriander or grated cheese.
Serve immediately with your favorite curries, kebabs, or dishes.

Enjoy your homemade naan! It’s a versatile bread that complements a wide range of dishes.
