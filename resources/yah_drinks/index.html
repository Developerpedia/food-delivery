Classic Homemade Lemonade Recipe

Ingredients

1 cup fresh lemon juice (about 4-6 lemons)
1 cup granulated sugar
4 cups cold water
Ice cubes
Lemon slices and mint leaves (for garnish, optional)

Instructions

1. Juice the Lemons: Squeeze the juice from the lemons into a measuring cup, making sure to remove any seeds. You should get about 1 cup of lemon juice. If you prefer, you can use a lemon juicer or reamer for efficiency.
2. Make the Simple Syrup: Combine Sugar and Water: In a small saucepan, combine 1 cup of sugar with 1 cup of water. Heat and Stir: Heat over medium heat, stirring continuously until the sugar is completely dissolved. This creates a simple syrup that will blend smoothly into the lemonade. Cool: Remove from heat and let it cool to room temperature.
3. Mix the Lemonade: Combine Ingredients: In a large pitcher, combine the fresh lemon juice, simple syrup (adjust to taste), and 3 cups of cold water. Stir well. Adjust Sweetness: Taste the lemonade and adjust the sweetness by adding more sugar or water if needed.
4. Serve: Add Ice: Fill glasses with ice cubes and pour the lemonade over the ice. Garnish: Garnish with lemon slices and mint leaves if desired.
5. Chill and Enjoy: For a colder drink, chill the lemonade in the refrigerator before serving.

Continue...

Mint Lemonade: Add a handful of fresh mint leaves to the pitcher and gently muddle them with a spoon to release their flavor before mixing.
Berry Lemonade: Blend a cup of fresh or frozen berries (such as strawberries, raspberries, or blueberries) and strain the mixture. Add this berry puree to the lemonade for a fruity twist.

Enjoy your refreshing homemade soft drink!
